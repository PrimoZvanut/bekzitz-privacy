Bekzitz Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Bekzitz is an Android app that reminds you to check the back seat when you leave your car. This policy explains what the app uses and what happens to it.

In short: Bekzitz does not send any data off your phone. It has no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics, and the app does not request the internet permission.

What the app uses, and why

Data / permissionWhy
Physical activity (activity recognition) and the step counter To notice that you started and finished a drive, and that you have walked away from the car. Activity recognition is provided by Google Play services on your device; the app receives only the result (for example "in vehicle" or "walking"). The app does not use your location and does not request any location permission.
Nearby devices (Bluetooth) Optional. With this permission the app sees when Bluetooth devices connect to and disconnect from your phone, so it can tell when you get into and out of the car you selected. The name and address of the cars you select are stored on your phone only.
Notifications, exact alarms and sound To show the "Child on board?" question and the "Check the back seat" reminder, and to repeat the reminder until you confirm. The reminder can speak aloud using the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone; that engine is provided by your phone maker or Google and is covered by its own privacy policy.
Foreground service, start at boot While you drive, a notification-backed service keeps the step counter running. After a restart or an app update the app re-registers drive detection.
Your choices Settings such as theme, reminder sound and volume, your own reminder texts if you write them, selected cars, and whether you answered "yes" to a trip. These are stored on your phone.

What is stored, and where

Everything above is stored locally in the app's private storage on your phone: the current trip state, your settings, the cars you selected, and a short technical log of drive detection events (times of detected drives and walking, step counts, and Bluetooth connect/disconnect events with the device name and only the last characters of its address). The log is capped at a small size and the oldest entries are overwritten. The published app has no way to export or send it.

This data is excluded from Android's cloud backup and device transfer. It does not leave your phone, and the developer cannot see it.

Sharing

Bekzitz does not share, sell or transmit personal data to anyone, and it contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

Google Play services, which is part of your Android system, provides activity recognition. Its handling of data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Support link

The app has an optional "Support" link. If you tap it, your web browser opens a page on ko-fi.com. That page is run by Ko-fi, not by Bekzitz, and is covered by its own privacy policy. Bekzitz sends nothing to Ko-fi.

Children

Bekzitz is meant for adults who drive. It does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Your control

  • You can withdraw any permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Bekzitz → Permissions. Drive detection then stops working.
  • Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling it, removes all data the app holds.

Safety notice

Bekzitz is a helper, not a safety device. It can miss a drive or fail to alert, for example when your phone restricts background apps. Always check the back seat yourself.
